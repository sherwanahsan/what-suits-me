# What suits me

User brief: a first product that helps someone choose clothing based on their skin tone, hair colour, height, body shape and preferred style.

One page, one action: fill in the five details, press the button, receive five colours, three cuts and three types of clothing with short reasons. Cuts is interpreted as garment cuts. Height uses centimetres. These are implementation assumptions from the brief.

No login, payments, deals, profile storage or result history. Backend and hosting use Convex. The OpenAI key is entered by the owner in each Convex deployment dashboard. The backend must enforce the shared hourly limit and return plain failure messages.

Visual direction: a calm lilac dressing-room worksheet, with a large conversational heading, tactile colour swatches and a clear white form. Results are suggestions rather than judgements about the user's body.
